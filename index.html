Creative Digital Curriculum

What Children Build at Folkestone Code Club

We follow the globally-renowned Code Club UK and Raspberry Pi Foundation pathway. Every child creates tangible projects they can play, test, and share with family at home.

Years 3 to 6

Scratch Game Design

Creating arcade classics, platform games, and seaside interactive animations using colorful drag-and-drop logic blocks.

  • Collision detection
  • Custom sound effects
  • Score counters & levels
Hardware & Gadgets

BBC micro:bit Inventions

Coding pocket computers with LED matrix screens, compasses, shake sensors, and radio antennas for secret Morse code.

  • Digital step counters
  • Rock-paper-scissors radio
  • Moisture alarms for plants
Years 5 & 6

Intro to Real Python

Moving from visual blocks to typed Python code. Building text adventure games, secret ciphers, and Turtle graphics art.

  • Variables & loops
  • Turtle geometric patterns
  • Caesar cipher decoders
Creative Web

HTML & CSS Web Design

Building their very first webpages about their favorite pets, sea creatures, or superheroes with real HTML markup and CSS colors.

  • Interactive buttons
  • Custom photo galleries
  • Styling with colorful CSS

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents and carers need to know before attending.

Does my child need any prior coding or computer experience?

None whatsoever! Many of our 7- and 8-year-olds join having never used a keyboard or mouse before. Our beginner modules guide children step-by-step with visual Scratch blocks, so every child progresses happily at their own pace.

Is the club really completely free? What is the catch?

Yes, 100% free! There are no hidden fees, subscriptions, or equipment charges. Folkestone Code Club is operated as a volunteer community initiative supported by Code Club UK, local STEM sponsors, and library partners to ensure every child has access to digital skills.

Do we need to bring our own laptop?

We provide club Chromebooks and laptops ready to use. If your child already has a personal laptop (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook) they prefer using, they are welcome to bring it along so they can continue their Scratch projects at home.

Do parents need to stay during the session?

For 7- and 8-year-olds (Year 3), we require a parent or carer to remain on premises. For older children in Years 4, 5, and 6, parents are welcome to stay, enjoy tea/coffee in our lounge, or pick up promptly at the end of the session.
