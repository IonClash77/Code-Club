Welcome to the Club

A Passionate Chess Community on the South Coast

Folkestone Chess Club has fostered camaraderie and competitive excellence for nearly a century. We are dedicated to creating an inclusive, stimulating environment for everyone from raw beginners to seasoned tournament champions.

Casual & Social Play

Drop in any Wednesday evening for untimed casual skirmishes, rapid chess, or blitz. Discuss classical games over a friendly drink in a relaxed setting.

  • No prior membership needed to visit
  • Standard and digital clocks provided
  • Welcoming to all adult ages
Competitive

Kent League Matches

We field multiple teams in the Kent County Chess Association (KCCA) across Divisions 1 and 3, plus internal handicap and rapidplay championships.

  • Officially ECF rated games
  • Home & away regional travel
  • Annual Folkestone Club Trophy

Junior Academy & Coaching

Structured early-evening junior sessions focused on tactical fundamentals, basic opening principles, and endgame technique supervised by club leaders.

  • Ages 7 to 17 welcome
  • DBS checked volunteers
  • Tactician puzzle awards

WEDNESDAYS JUNIOR CLUB

Junior Chess Academy

6:00 PM – 7:15 PM

Targeted at players aged 7 to 17. Mini coaching clinics, tactic challenges, and beginner friendly games with experienced tutors.

Adults & Open Club Night

7:30 PM – 10:30 PM

The heartbeat of our club. Social rapid games, clock duels, casual untimed matches, and officially scheduled Kent County League games.

Guests and visitors welcome with zero fees

Venue Location

The Leas Club Room, Folkestone

Located just steps from Folkestone’s iconic cliff-top Leas promenade, offering comfortable, well-lit, quiet playing spaces with seaside views.

The Leas, Folkestone, Kent, CT20 2XL (Ground floor west wing)
Folkestone Central Station: 10–12 minute walk (Direct 54-minute train from London St Pancras via HS1).
Parking: On-street parking free after 6:00 PM along Clifton Crescent and The Leas.
